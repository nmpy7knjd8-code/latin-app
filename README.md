# Latin through Rome & the Medieval West

**Live site: https://nmpy7knjd8-code.github.io/latin-app/**

A Duolingo-style Latin learning app built around real, source-checked quotations, history cards about Rome and the medieval West, FSRS spaced-repetition ideas, and pronunciation guides (restored classical; medieval/ecclesiastical where noted).

- `cards/` – card viewer (Learn / Review modes, tap-to-flip, word glosses, IPA + respelling)
- `content/` – card data: `rome-cards.json`, `medieval-cards.json`, `learning-cards.json`, `phonetics.json`
- `audio/` – human recordings of selected phrases (see `audio-sources.md`)
- `research.md`, `game-design.md`, `review-*.md` – research, design and dialectical reviews

Work in progress: content is still being added and checked.
